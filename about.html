Our story

The classroom is only the beginning.

We noticed a big gap between classroom education and real-world industry requirements. Talented students had the ambition, but not always the opportunity to practise what they learned.

That’s why we created Elevate — a space where BE and BTech students build confidence through hands-on experience, real projects and a community that moves forward together.
