FOUNDER PROFILE

Sagar Varma

Founder & CEO

Sagar Varma is the Founder & CEO, driven by a vision to transform the education landscape through technology, innovation, and industry-focused learning. With expertise in Full Stack Development, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Data Analytics, he is passionate about building an educational ecosystem that prepares individuals for the demands of the modern technology industry.

With a strong foundation in technical education, software development, and professional training, he believes that learning should go beyond theoretical knowledge. His approach emphasizes practical implementation, real-world projects, problem-solving, industry exposure, and continuous skill development.

Under his leadership, the organization aims to bridge the gap between academic education and industry expectations by providing quality technical training, hands-on internships, mentorship, certification programs, and career-oriented learning opportunities.
